01 / ABOUT ME

Driven by curiosity. Grounded in evidence.

For me, astrophysics is where deep questions meet the discipline of careful observation.

I'm pursuing a PhD in Astrophysics at Universiti Sains Malaysia, specialising in solar physics (solar flare) and the analysis of X-ray satellite data. My work brings together observational astronomy, scientific programming, and statistical thinking to better understand how solar flares evolve.

Beyond the Sun, my interests extend to gravitational waves, black holes, cosmology, space technology, and science outreach. I enjoy making complex ideas accessible and finding opportunities to collaborate across disciplines.
